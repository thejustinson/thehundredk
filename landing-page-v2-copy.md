## 9. Frequently asked questions

### Is the consultation really free?

Yes. You can start with a WhatsApp conversation, and we'll arrange a free consultation when you're ready to discuss your requirements and define the project.

### Does ₦99,999 cover everything?

It covers the deliverables agreed for your package. Hosting, third-party subscriptions, additional work, and other costs are included only if explicitly stated in your project agreement.

### What if I don't have a domain?

We can subsidise an eligible domain purchase by up to ₦10,000. If the domain costs more, we'll explain the difference before you commit. Renewal costs are your responsibility unless agreed otherwise.

### What does the two-month support cover?

Support covers assistance with the delivered work within the agreed terms. It doesn't automatically include new features, a redesign, unlimited content updates, or new projects.

### How soon can you deliver?

Eligible projects target delivery within five business days after the scope and payment are confirmed and all required materials are received. We'll confirm your timeline before starting.

### What if I don't know exactly what my business needs?

That's fine. Tell us about your business and what you're trying to achieve. We'll help you identify a practical starting point during the free consultation.

### Can I become a partner if I don't own a business?

Yes. You can join as a partner and introduce businesses or individuals who may need our services, subject to approval under the partner programme.

### How do I earn commission?

You earn 20% of qualifying project payments actually received for customers attributed to your referral. Clicks and enquiries alone do not generate commission.
