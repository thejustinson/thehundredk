# 1. Executive Summary

THEHUNDREDK is a business growth and digital enablement brand that helps small businesses, professionals, creators, and individuals establish a stronger online presence and improve how they manage their operations.

The business launches with a ₦99,999 package that combines website development or online presence improvements with business data organisation, client management improvements, professional email guidance, a free pre-project consultation, two months of post-project support, and a domain subsidy of up to ₦10,000 for eligible customers who do not already have a suitable domain.

THEHUNDREDK is not intended to be positioned as just another website development agency. Its role is closer to that of a **Business Growth Partner**: helping customers identify their needs, choose suitable digital tools, improve their business processes, and establish a more professional digital presence.

The platform will have three interconnected systems:

1. **Public website and customer acquisition:** communicates the offer, captures enquiries, records referral attribution, and directs prospects into a WhatsApp conversation.
2. **Partner platform:** enables people to introduce customers, access marketing materials, track leads, and monitor their commissions.
3. **Internal operations platform:** enables the administrator to manage leads, customer communication, bookings, projects, payments, partner attribution, commissions, payouts, and marketing assets.

The primary distribution strategy is to recruit partners who share THEHUNDREDK with their networks. Customers enter through partner links or manually supplied partner IDs. Their information is recorded before they continue to WhatsApp, where the business establishes direct human contact and progresses the opportunity toward a free consultation, agreed project scope, payment, and delivery.

The system must prioritise speed, simplicity, attribution accuracy, and transparent commission accounting.

# 7. Customer Acquisition and Referral Flow

## 7.1 Overview

The intended flow is:

Partner invites prospect → prospect opens referral link → prospect completes enquiry form → lead is saved → Telegram notification is generated → prospect continues to WhatsApp → human conversation begins → consultation is scheduled when appropriate → scope is agreed → project payment is recorded → delivery begins.

The website captures and organises the lead. WhatsApp enables direct human engagement. The internal system tracks the relationship and its commercial outcome.

## 7.2 Referral links

Every approved partner receives a unique referral code and URL.

Example:

`https://thehundredk.fun/start?via=JUSTIN20`

The code is an identifier, not proof of payment or entitlement to a commission.

When the visitor arrives through a valid link, the application should:

1. Read the supplied referral code.
2. Validate it against an active partner record.
3. Store the validated referral information in localStorage.
4. Display the referring partner's name and identifier in the form.
5. Preserve attribution for subsequent visits in that browser.
6. Associate the attribution with the lead when the form is submitted.

If a visitor opens a newer valid referral link, the system updates the current referral according to the last-valid-referral-wins policy.

The application must not trust arbitrary browser-supplied partner IDs without server validation.

## 7.3 Manual partner ID

Visitors who receive a partner ID without a link must be able to enter it manually.

Requirements:

- The field accepts a partner code.
- The code is validated by the server.
- The partner's name is displayed when validation succeeds.
- Invalid codes are flagged and cannot silently generate commission.
- Once the visitor submits the form, the validated attribution is persisted.

## 7.4 Referral attribution policy

The initial model is last valid partner referral wins.

The system should maintain an attribution history for operational analysis and dispute resolution, while assigning one primary partner to each qualifying sale.

LocalStorage is browser-specific and can be cleared. Consequently, attribution should be persisted on the server as soon as the prospect submits their details.

If a prospect returns on another device or uses a different browser, the original attribution may not be recoverable automatically. The system should support manual attribution corrections where there is sufficient evidence.

The initial attribution window is 30 days, subject to the final published partner terms.

## 7.5 Enquiry form

The form is the primary entry point for partner-referred leads.

Required fields:

- Brand or business name.
- Contact person's full name.
- Email address.
- Service interest.
- Referring partner ID or validated referral.

Recommended service-interest options:

- Website or online presence.
- Online store.
- Business data and client management.
- Automation and email services.
- Not sure yet.

Additional fields may be introduced if they improve qualification without making the form unnecessarily long.

The customer's contact details and referral information should be saved to the database before the application opens WhatsApp.

The form should validate required fields, email format, referral code, and duplicate submissions.

## 7.6 WhatsApp-first contact

After successful form submission, the visitor sees a clear button to continue to WhatsApp.

The website generates a prefilled message using the customer's submitted information and validated partner attribution.

Message template:

"Hi, this is [Full Name] from [Brand Name].

I'm reaching out to THEHUNDREDK because I'm interested in [Selected Service].

We were referred by [Partner Name].

I'd like to discuss how we can work together."

The message should be URL-encoded and opened through a WhatsApp click-to-chat link.

The application must not assume the message has been sent merely because WhatsApp was opened. The customer must press Send.

The lead should initially be marked as "Enquiry submitted" or "Awaiting WhatsApp contact". The administrator can subsequently mark the lead as contacted.

A later WhatsApp integration may support deeper message-status automation, but it is not required for launch.

# 8. Telegram Notifications

## 8.1 Purpose

Telegram is the administrator's immediate notification channel for new leads and important operational events.

It is an internal notification mechanism, not the primary customer communication channel.

## 8.2 New lead alert

On successful form submission, the system queues a Telegram notification containing:

- Lead reference.
- Brand name.
- Contact name.
- Email address.
- Service interest.
- Partner name and code.
- Referral source.
- Submission timestamp.
- Current lead status.
- Link to the protected internal lead record.

The alert should be concise and actionable.

## 8.3 Additional notifications

The system should notify the administrator about:

- New partner applications.
- New bookings.
- Booking cancellations.
- Verified project payments.
- Manual conversion records.
- Commission entries requiring review.
- Partner payout records.
- Payment or booking reconciliation issues.
- Important notification delivery failures.

Notifications must not be treated as the authoritative business record. The database remains the source of truth.

## 8.4 Reliability

Notifications should be handled through a retryable outbox or job queue.

If Telegram is unavailable, the lead must remain saved. The system should retry the notification and log the failure.

# 9. Sales Process and Lead Management

## 9.1 Human-led sales

THEHUNDREDK does not require an automated proposal workflow for every customer.

After a prospect reaches out through WhatsApp, the administrator responds, understands the customer's needs, and determines the appropriate next step.

The process should be flexible enough to handle customers who already know what they want and those who need guidance.

## 9.2 Lead statuses

Recommended lead lifecycle:

- New.
- Contacted.
- Consultation to be scheduled.
- Consultation booked.
- Scope being defined.
- Scope agreed.
- Awaiting payment.
- Converted.
- Lost.

"Proposal sent" should be an optional event, not a mandatory stage.

A proposal or formal quote is required only when the work's scope, price, or terms need to be documented or agreed separately.

## 9.3 Manual lead updates

The administrator must be able to:

- Open a lead.
- Review the submitted information.
- See the referral attribution.
- Add internal notes.
- Record WhatsApp contact.
- Schedule a consultation.
- Record agreed scope.
- Attach or reference a quote or proposal if needed.
- Record the agreed project price.
- Record payment details.
- Mark a lead as converted or lost.

Every important status change should be timestamped and associated with the user or process that performed it.

# 10. Consultation and Booking System

## 10.1 Free consultation policy

Consultations are free.

There is no separate ₦9,999 consultation charge.

The consultation happens after the initial WhatsApp exchange, when the customer and administrator are ready to discuss and finalise the project scope.

It is not necessary for every prospect to book a call immediately after submitting the enquiry form.

## 10.2 Booking flow

1. The administrator and customer communicate through WhatsApp.
2. Once the customer is qualified and ready to define scope, a consultation is arranged.
3. The customer is offered available time slots.
4. The customer selects a slot.
5. The system records the booking and creates the calendar event.
6. A Google Meet link is generated when an online meeting is required.
7. The customer receives confirmation.
8. The administrator receives a Telegram notification.

The system may support administrator-created bookings when the customer and administrator agree on a time directly through WhatsApp.

## 10.3 Calendar integration

Use a dedicated THEHUNDREDK Google Calendar to control availability.

The booking system must:

- Check actual availability before offering a slot.
- Prevent overlapping active bookings.
- Create calendar events after successful booking.
- Generate a Google Meet link when applicable.
- Store the calendar event ID and meeting URL.
- Send the customer the confirmed date, time, timezone, and joining link.
- Support cancellation and rescheduling.
- Handle failed calendar event creation without falsely confirming a completed booking.

All booking times should be stored consistently and displayed in the appropriate timezone, initially Africa/Lagos.

## 10.4 Booking management

Customers do not need an account to book a consultation.

A secure booking reference or protected management link may be used for viewing or changing an existing booking.

The system should support administrator-led scheduling as well as customer self-service booking.

- Assigned partner attribution.
- Agreed deliverables.
- Project amount.
- Agreed timeline.
- Payment terms.
- Project status.
- Domain subsidy, if applicable.

- Support start and end dates.

## 11.2 Project lifecycle

Recommended project statuses:

- Scope definition.
- Scope agreed.
- Awaiting payment.
- In progress.
- Awaiting customer input.
- Delivered.
- Support period active.
- Support period completed.
- Cancelled or refunded, where applicable.

The system does not need to become a complete project management suite. It should record the information necessary to manage customer relationships, revenue, delivery, and support obligations.

## 11.3 Payments

Paystack should be used for supported online payments.

The system must:

- Create payment requests from the agreed project amount.
- Verify payments server-side.
- Validate payment references, amounts, currency, and provider responses.
- Process webhooks idempotently.
- Record refunds or payment reversals.
- Avoid marking a project as paid based only on a browser redirect.
- Support recording verified payments made through other agreed channels where necessary.

For a project priced at ₦99,999, the qualifying amount and any applicable domain subsidy or other adjustment must be represented clearly in the project record.

## 11.4 Domain subsidy accounting

The system should record:

- Whether the customer already owns a suitable domain.
- Whether the customer qualifies for the subsidy.
- Domain name and registration provider.
- Actual eligible domain cost.
- Subsidy amount applied.
- Customer contribution, if any.
- Renewal date or responsibility, where available.

The subsidy cannot exceed ₦10,000.

The business must define whether the subsidy is applied directly to a domain purchase, reimbursed against evidence, or provided through another documented mechanism.

# 13. Partner Programme

## 13.1 Programme purpose

The partner programme is the primary distribution mechanism for THEHUNDREDK.

Partners help the business reach potential customers by sharing its services, introducing prospects, and making their networks aware of the offer.

Partners do not need to be professional salespeople. They should have an easy way to share the service, introduce interested people, and understand the outcome of their efforts.

## 13.2 Partner recruitment

The public `/partners` page explains the programme and accepts applications.

The onboarding process is:

1. A person discovers the programme.
2. They submit an application.
3. The administrator reviews the application.
4. Approved applicants receive access.
5. The partner receives a unique referral link and ID.
6. The partner accesses marketing materials and begins distribution.

The platform must support approval, rejection, suspension, and account recovery.

## 13.3 Informal partner invitations

Partners may tell friends or colleagues that they are participating in THEHUNDREDK and invite them to join.

Recruitment is informal and does not require a visible hierarchy in the dashboard.

There are no recruitment bonuses or downline commissions in the initial model.

The platform should not build a recruitment tree, recruit leaderboard, or multilevel compensation structure.

If partner application sources are useful, the application may optionally record how an applicant discovered the programme. This is for analytics, not recruitment-based compensation.

## 13.4 Partner commission

The initial commission rate is 20% of qualifying project payments attributed to the partner.

For a qualifying project payment of ₦99,999:

- Project payment: ₦99,999.
- Partner commission: ₦19,999.80.
- Agency balance before costs and applicable taxes: ₦79,999.20.

The final programme terms must define the treatment of refunds, chargebacks, discounts, partial payments, and other adjustments.

Commission should not be calculated twice for the same economic value. Where a payment is credited toward a project or adjusted under the agreed terms, the ledger should reflect the qualifying transaction correctly.

Recruiting another partner does not generate commission.

## 13.5 Attribution rules

The system must support:

- Automatic attribution from referral links.
- Manual attribution from a partner ID.
- Manual customer introductions.
- Server-side partner validation.
- Attribution history.
- Attribution corrections with an audit trail.
- Persistence of attribution across the lead, project, and payment records.

The initial model uses last valid partner referral wins, subject to the 30-day attribution window and published terms.

Only one primary partner receives commission for a qualifying sale under the initial model. Multi-touch commission sharing is outside the first release.

# 16. Data Model

The application should use Supabase Postgres as the primary database.

Core entities:

- `contacts`: customer and prospect contact records.
- `leads`: enquiries, source, service interest, status, and sales progress.
- `partners`: partner identity, application status, and programme membership.
- `partner_attributions`: validated attribution records and attribution history.
- `bookings`: consultation and discovery bookings.
- `projects`: agreed project scope, pricing, timeline, delivery, and support.
- `payments`: provider references, amounts, payment status, and verification.
- `commission_ledger`: qualifying transactions, commission amounts, adjustments, and statuses.
- `payouts`: partner payout records.
- `partner_assets`: marketing material metadata and publication status.
- `partner_asset_downloads`: optional download records.
- `lead_events`: auditable history of lead status changes.
- `webhook_events`: provider webhook processing and idempotency.
- `notification_outbox`: pending and completed notification jobs.

Additional fields should support domain subsidy records, support-period tracking, and project completion dates.

All records should use stable identifiers, timestamps, and appropriate foreign keys. Financial amounts should be stored as integer minor currency units, such as kobo, rather than floating-point values.

# 18. Security and Privacy

The application must enforce permissions at the server and database levels.

Requirements:

- Enable Row Level Security on applicable Supabase tables.
- Derive partner identity from the authenticated session.
- Never trust a browser-supplied partner ID as proof of access.
- Never expose service-role credentials to the browser.
- Protect administrator routes and operations with verified authorisation.
- Validate and sanitise submitted data.
- Rate-limit forms and sensitive endpoints.
- Validate referral codes against active partner records.
- Verify payment webhooks and process them idempotently.
- Protect booking management links.
- Use appropriate access controls for private marketing assets.
- Minimise personal information exposed to partners.
- Define retention and deletion procedures for personal data.
- Record sensitive administrative changes in an audit trail.

# 19. Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance

Public pages and forms should load quickly on typical Nigerian mobile connections.

## Reliability

Lead records must persist even if WhatsApp or Telegram is unavailable.

## Accessibility

Forms, controls, status messages, and navigation should be accessible and usable on mobile devices.

## Scalability

The system should support more partners and leads without requiring a complete redesign.

## Maintainability

Business rules, attribution logic, commission calculations, and notification processing should be implemented in reusable server-side modules.

## Observability

# 21. Acceptance Criteria

The product is ready for launch when the following scenarios work correctly.

### Customer acquisition

- A visitor opens a valid partner link and sees the correct partner attribution.
- A visitor can manually enter a valid partner ID.
- An invalid partner ID cannot silently earn commission.
- The visitor can submit the form successfully.
- The lead and attribution are saved before the WhatsApp handoff.
- The generated WhatsApp message contains the submitted identity, selected service, and referring partner.
- A Telegram alert is queued for the new lead.
- Notification failures do not delete the lead.
- A returning visitor retains their last valid referral in the same browser.

### Consultation and project workflow

- The administrator can contact the customer and update the lead status.
- A free consultation can be scheduled after initial qualification.
- Calendar availability prevents conflicting bookings.
- The administrator can record agreed scope without creating a mandatory proposal.
- A project can be created and marked paid only after appropriate verification.
- The domain subsidy cannot exceed ₦10,000.
- The two-month support period is calculated from project completion.

### Partner platform

- Approved partners can sign in and access only their own authorised records.
- Each partner has a unique referral link and ID.
- Partners can download published marketing materials.
- Partners can view attributed leads and commission statuses.
- Partners cannot approve their own commissions or view unrelated partner records.
- Recruiting another partner does not create a commission entry.

### Commission accounting

- A qualifying paid project creates no more than one commission entry per partner and qualifying transaction.
- Manual and automated conversion events cannot double-count the same payment.
- Refunds and reversals update the ledger according to policy.
- Payouts are recorded with amounts, dates, and references.
- Partners can distinguish pending, approved, paid, and reversed commission.

### Security and reliability

- Sensitive API operations require authorisation.
- Payment webhooks are verified and idempotent.
- Booking conflicts are prevented.
- Private assets are protected.
- Critical errors are logged and can be reconciled.
